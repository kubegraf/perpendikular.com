Perspectives

One question. Every angle.

Complex decisions rarely have one correct perspective. Perpendikular deliberately separates the problem into independent viewpoints before bringing them back together.

  • Market

    Demand, timing, segment size and where the pull actually comes from.

  • Finance

    Unit economics, payback period, cash exposure and downside cost.

  • Technology

    Feasibility, delivery risk and the cost of the architecture you commit to.

  • Legal

    Jurisdiction, contracts, liability and the regulatory load you inherit.

  • Security

    Threat surface, data residency and the controls the decision assumes.

  • Customer

    Real willingness to pay, switching cost and stated versus revealed need.

  • Operations

    Who runs it on day 200, and what that complexity costs to staff.

  • Competition

    Incumbents, indirect alternatives and the response you should expect.

  • Strategy

    Option value, reversibility and whether this compounds or constrains.

  • Risk

    Failure modes ranked by likelihood, blast radius and time to detect.

Perspectives run independently — no shared context, no consensus bias.

Contradiction engine

Find where the story breaks.

Perpendikular compares independent perspectives against each other and flags the places where their conclusions cannot all be true at once.

  • Finance says

    “US launch has better revenue potential.”

    Larger addressable spend, higher contract values.

  • Operations says

    “UK launch has lower operational complexity.”

    Single jurisdiction, one support timezone, no state-level variance.

  • Market analysis says

    “US acquisition costs are significantly higher.”

    Paid channels are 2.4× more expensive in the target segment.

Contradiction detected

Revenue potential is higher in the US, but the assumed acquisition economics may invalidate the advantage.
